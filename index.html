Why AI feels dumb

You may be testing the weakest version of the thing.

Free / base lane

10 messages can fool you.

  • ChatGPT Free: up to 10 GPT-5.5 messages every 5 hours.
  • Free context window: 16K tokens.
  • After limits, chats fall back to mini.
  • Older GPT-4o / GPT-4.1 ChatGPT models retired Feb. 13, 2026.
OpenAI frontier

GPT-5.5 Pro is a different test.

  • GPT-5.5 Thinking: up to 400K context on Pro.
  • GPT-5.5 Pro API: 1.05M context, 128K max output.
  • Terminal-Bench 2.0: 82.7% vs GPT-5.4 at 75.1%.
  • Codex puts the model inside files, commands, and tests.
Claude frontier

Sonnet is not Opus.

  • Claude Code Pro: about 10-40 prompts every 5 hours.
  • Claude Max 20x: about 200-800 Claude Code prompts.
  • Opus 4.7: 1M context window for large work.
  • CursorBench: Opus 4.7 at 70% vs Opus 4.6 at 58%.
Official OpenAI + Anthropic examples, May 2026. Test the real tier before judging the category.5 / 17

A general AI chat assistant from OpenAI.

What it does

Drafts, analyzes, explains, codes, and reasons with the context you provide.

What it does not do

The missing signal

Most companies use tools but fail to capture what happened. The feedback loop is what turns one-off AI usage into company intelligence.

  • What did the AI suggest?
  • What did staff accept or change?
  • What was the business outcome?
  • What should the system do differently next time?
Usage without feedback is a wasted learning signal.9 / 17
